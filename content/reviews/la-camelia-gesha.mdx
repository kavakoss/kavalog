## Tasting Notes

A light‑bodied cup with a tea‑like quality and soft citrus acidity, reminiscent of some of our favourite Ethiopian profiles.

---

## Brewing Notes

This is a delicate washed Gesha — treat it gently.

| Method | Grind (X‑Ultra) | Grind (K‑Ultra) | Ratio | Temp |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V60 (3‑pour) | `1.5.0 – 2.0.0` | 78–82 clicks | 1:16 | 93–95°C |
| V60 (4:6) | `2.4.0 – 2.6.0` | 100–105 clicks | 1:15 | 93°C |

### Key tips

- **No agitation** — Washed Gesha over‑extracts easily. Swirl gently, never stir.
- **Let it cool** — The tea‑like character and citrus notes become dominant around 50–60°C.
- **Water matters** — Use low‑mineral water (Aqua or TWW Light). High TDS water will mute the delicate florals.
- **Finer grind, higher temp** — Light roast + washed process = hard to extract. Don't be shy with the temperature.
