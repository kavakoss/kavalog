## Overview

The **1Zpresso X‑Ultra** sits between the espresso‑focused J‑Ultra and the filter‑leaning K‑Ultra — a true all‑rounder with a 40mm heptagonal burr, 12.5µm per click, and external numbered adjustment dial.

---

## Specs

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| Burr | 40mm heptagonal stainless steel |
| Step Size | 12.5 microns per click |
| Capacity | 25–30g |
| Weight | 590g |
| Adjustment | External dial, 60 clicks/rotation |
| Magnetic Catch Cup | Yes |
| Foldable Handle | Yes |
| Colours | Midnight Black, Pacific Blue, Space Gray |

---

## What I Like

- **Incredible grind consistency** — near‑zero fines for pour over
- **Magnetic catch cup** — huge upgrade over threaded
- **Easy calibration** — pop off dial, realign to zero
- **Versatile** — Turkish coffee to French press
- **Compact & travel‑ready** — includes hard case
- **Value** — punches above its price point

## What I Don't Like

- **Not a specialist** — good at everything, great at nothing
- **Smooth body** — can be slippery when grinding for espresso
- **Narrow espresso range** — only ~20 clicks of usable range
- **Early batch recall** — first batch had burr chipping (all recalled and fixed)
