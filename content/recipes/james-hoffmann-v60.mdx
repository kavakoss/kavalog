## Overview

James Hoffmann's **ultimate V60 technique** — simple, repeatable, and designed to maximize even extraction. Uses a 1:16.67 ratio (slightly weaker than standard) to improve clarity, plus a post‑brew swirl for a flat, even coffee bed.

---

## The Key Points

- **Wetter ratio** (1:16.67) — better flavor clarity, less intensity
- **Bloom with swirling** — no spoon, just gentle rotation
- **Single main pour** (after bloom) — up to 60% of total water in one go
- **Post‑brew swirl** — flattens the bed, prevents doming

---

## Recipe

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Coffee | **15g** |
| Water | **250ml** |
| Temperature | **93–96°C** (light roast) / **90–93°C** (medium) |
| Grind (X‑Ultra) | **`2.0.0` – `2.2.0`** |
| Grind (K‑Ultra) | **78 – 86 clicks** |
| Filter | Hario V60 paper (rinsed) |

### Steps

```
1. Rinse filter, discard water. Add grounds, level the bed.
2. 0:00 — Pour ~35–40ml, swirl vigorously. Bloom 30–45s.
3. 0:30–0:45 — Pour steadily to 150ml (60% of total).
   Aim for a gentle, center‑focused stream.
4. 1:15 — Pour the remaining 100ml slowly.
5. Once water has drained ~1cm above the bed,
   gently swirl the dripper to flatten the bed.
6. Let drawdown complete. Target: 2:30 – 3:15.
```

### Tips

- **Swirl, don't stir:** Hoffmann explicitly recommends swirling — no spoons.
- **Wait for the drawdown:** Don't pull the dripper early; let all water drain.
- **Flat bed = even extraction:** If the bed is domed or uneven after brewing, your pour technique needs work.
