## Overview

Tetsuya Kasuya's **2026 "Multi‑Pour" method** (also called "Neo Brew" or "Click 45") throws out the fine‑grind trend and goes **super coarse** with **ten 30g pours**. The payoff? Thick, syrupy body, dominant sweetness, and almost zero bitterness.

> Released to mark the 10th anniversary of his 2016 World Brewers Cup win.

---

## Why It Works

- **Super coarse grind** → slow extraction, less surface area
- **96°C water** → pushes extraction despite coarse particles
- **10 pours** → multiple percolation cycles build body and texture

The result: a cup that tastes thick, almost jelly‑like as it cools, with sweetness dominating and acidity taking a back seat.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Coffee | **20g** |
| Water | **300ml** (1:15) |

### Pour Schedule

```
0:00  — 30ml — bloom, gentle swirl, 30s
0:30  — 30ml (total 60ml)
0:45  — 30ml (total 90ml)
1:00  — 30ml (total 120ml)
1:15  — 30ml (total 150ml)
1:30  — 30ml (total 180ml)
1:45  — 30ml (total 210ml)
2:00  — 30ml (total 240ml)
2:15  — 30ml (total 270ml)
2:30  — 30ml (total 300ml)  ← final pour
~3:30 — drawdown complete
```

### Tips

- **Keep the cadence:** One pour every 15 seconds. Use a timer or metronome.
- **Water must be HOT:** 95–96°C is non‑negotiable. This drives extraction.
- **Let it cool:** The cup transforms as it cools — wait 2–3 minutes before judging.
- **Best for:** Light & medium‑light single origins. Avoid dark roasts (bitter).
- **If it's too thin:** Grind 3–5 clicks finer, or swirl the server gently mid‑brew.
